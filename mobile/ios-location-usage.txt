Add these keys to the iOS Info.plist when syncing Capacitor (Bump / nearby):

NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription
  Nexus uses your location to find friends and family nearby with Bump. Location is rounded and expires after 20 minutes.

NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription
  Nexus can keep Bump updated while you use the app so family can find you nearby.

After `npm i` in mobile/, run `npx cap sync ios` so @capacitor/geolocation is linked.
